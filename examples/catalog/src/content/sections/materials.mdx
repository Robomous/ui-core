## Surface

A surface on the page stays in place. A raised surface gets `ring-1 ring-foreground/10`, never a
coloured border: the ring reads as a hairline in light mode and as a faint glint in dark mode,
with no second token. Each sample sits on what it would sit on in a product, so even a surface the
colour of the page still shows its edges.

<MaterialRows set="surface" />

## Floating

A floating surface sits above the page. Anything that opens and closes paints on `popover`, so it
switches between light and dark on its own. The more modal it is, the more lift it gets. A Tooltip
has no shadow at all; a Dialog gets the most, a long soft shadow over a light scrim.

## Radius

`--radius: 0.625rem` is set in the stylesheet, and every step is derived from it in
`@theme inline`, from `radius-sm` at ×0.6 to `radius-4xl` at ×2.6. Components take their radius
from that scale. A surface nested inside another takes the next step down, so the corners stay
concentric.

The exception is an arbitrary value left in a component. That is geometry, not radius: a one-off
measurement that doesn't belong to any scale.

## Focus and invalid

Focus and invalid are materials too: a stroke and a ring painted over a control's resting surface.
Each focusable component draws its own ring, and invalid wears the same geometry in the
destructive colour. [States](/docs/states) covers what sets each one.

## Best practices

### Choosing a material

- Reach for a material instead of hand-rolling a radius, a fill, a stroke and a shadow. The preset
  already encodes where the surface sits.
- Pick it by where the element sits: `card` for content in place, `popover` for what floats, the
  scrim for what is modal.
- Don't stack two materials on one element. A child that needs more lift becomes a surface of its
  own.

### Restraint

- Use the lowest elevation that still reads as raised against its background. Too much elevation
  is the usual source of visual noise.
- Status is never a stroke on a container. A status `Badge` paints its surface and keeps
  `border-transparent`; an `Alert` keeps a neutral hairline and puts the status colour on its icon
  alone.
- Let the material set the chrome and leave spacing to the layout. Don't override a surface's
  shadow to make room.

### Accessibility

- A material is decorative. The meaning lives on the element that carries the role, such as
  `role="dialog"` on a Dialog or `role="tooltip"` on a Tooltip.
- Elevation never stands in for focus. Every focusable surface also shows the focus-visible ring.
- Check a material in both themes. Shadows read weaker on a dark page than on a light one, so make
  sure the separation still shows.
